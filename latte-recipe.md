# Classic Latte Recipe

A delicious and creamy latte that you can make at home!

## Ingredients

- 2 shots espresso (about 2 oz / 60 ml)
- 8-10 oz (240-300 ml) steamed milk
- Thin layer of milk foam (about 1/4 inch)
- Optional: sugar, flavored syrup, or sweetener to taste

## Equipment

- Espresso machine or moka pot
- Milk frother or steam wand
- 12 oz latte cup or mug
- Thermometer (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Brew the Espresso**
   - Pull 2 shots of espresso directly into your latte cup
   - The espresso should have a rich, golden crema on top

2. **Steam the Milk**
   - Pour cold milk into a steaming pitcher (fill to just below the spout)
   - Heat and froth the milk to 150-155°F (65-68°C)
   - Create a velvety microfoam by positioning the steam wand just below the surface
   - Tap the pitcher on the counter to remove large bubbles

3. **Combine**
   - Hold the pitcher at an angle
   - Pour the steamed milk slowly into the espresso
   - Start pouring from about 2-3 inches above the cup
   - As the cup fills, bring the pitcher closer to create latte art (optional)

4. **Serve**
   - Top with a thin layer of foam
   - Add sweetener if desired
   - Enjoy immediately while hot!

## Tips

- Use whole milk for the creamiest texture
- Keep your milk between 150-155°F to preserve sweetness
- Practice your pour for beautiful latte art
- Experiment with flavored syrups like vanilla, caramel, or hazelnut

## Variations

- **Iced Latte**: Pour espresso over ice and add cold milk
- **Vanilla Latte**: Add 1-2 pumps of vanilla syrup
- **Mocha Latte**: Add 1-2 tablespoons of chocolate syrup
- **Oat Milk Latte**: Substitute oat milk for a dairy-free option

Enjoy your homemade latte! ☕
