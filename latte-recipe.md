# Classic Latte Recipe

A delicious and creamy latte that you can make at home.

## Ingredients

- 2 shots (2 oz / 60 ml) of espresso
- 8 oz (240 ml) of whole milk
- Optional: sugar or sweetener to taste
- Optional: flavored syrup (vanilla, caramel, etc.)

## Equipment

- Espresso machine or moka pot
- Milk frother or steam wand
- 12 oz latte cup or mug
- Thermometer (optional but helpful)

## Instructions

### 1. Brew the Espresso
- Grind fresh coffee beans to a fine consistency
- Pull 2 shots of espresso (about 2 oz)
- Pour the espresso into your latte cup

### 2. Steam the Milk
- Pour cold milk into a steaming pitcher
- Insert the steam wand just below the milk's surface
- Steam the milk to 150-155°F (65-68°C)
- Create microfoam by incorporating air during the first few seconds
- Continue steaming until the milk is silky and smooth

### 3. Combine
- Pour the steamed milk slowly into the espresso
- Hold back the foam with a spoon initially
- Then spoon the remaining foam on top
- For latte art, pour the milk in a steady stream from a height, then bring closer to create patterns

### 4. Serve
- Serve immediately while hot
- Optionally dust with cocoa powder or cinnamon

## Tips

- Use fresh, high-quality coffee beans for the best flavor
- Whole milk creates the creamiest texture, but you can use any milk alternative
- The ideal temperature for steamed milk is 150-155°F - too hot and it scorches
- Practice your pouring technique for beautiful latte art
- Clean your steam wand immediately after use

## Variations

- **Vanilla Latte**: Add 1 oz vanilla syrup before pouring milk
- **Caramel Latte**: Add caramel syrup and top with caramel drizzle
- **Iced Latte**: Pour espresso and cold milk over ice
- **Mocha Latte**: Add chocolate syrup or cocoa powder

Enjoy your homemade latte! ☕
