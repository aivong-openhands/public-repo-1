# Classic Latte Recipe

A delicious and creamy latte that you can make at home.

## Ingredients

- 2 shots of espresso (about 2 oz / 60 ml)
- 8-10 oz (240-300 ml) of steamed milk
- Thin layer of milk foam (about 1/4 inch)

## Equipment Needed

- Espresso machine or Moka pot
- Milk frother or steam wand
- Coffee mug (12 oz capacity)
- Thermometer (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Espresso**
   - Grind fresh coffee beans to a fine consistency
   - Pull 2 shots of espresso into your mug
   - The espresso should take about 25-30 seconds to extract

2. **Steam the Milk**
   - Pour cold milk into a frothing pitcher
   - Heat milk to 150-155°F (65-68°C) using the steam wand
   - Create microfoam by keeping the steam wand just below the milk surface
   - Swirl the milk to achieve a silky, smooth texture

3. **Combine**
   - Pour the steamed milk over the espresso
   - Hold back the foam with a spoon initially
   - Top with a thin layer of foam
   - Optional: Create latte art by pouring carefully

4. **Serve**
   - Enjoy immediately while hot
   - Optional: dust with cocoa powder or cinnamon

## Tips

- Use whole milk for the creamiest texture
- Fresh espresso is key to a great latte
- Practice your milk steaming technique for better microfoam
- The ideal milk temperature is 150-155°F - avoid overheating

## Variations

- **Vanilla Latte**: Add 1-2 pumps of vanilla syrup
- **Caramel Latte**: Add caramel syrup and top with caramel drizzle
- **Iced Latte**: Pour espresso over ice and add cold milk instead of steamed milk
- **Oat Milk Latte**: Substitute with oat milk for a dairy-free option

Enjoy your homemade latte! ☕
