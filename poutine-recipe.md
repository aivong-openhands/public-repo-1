# Classic Poutine Recipe

A beloved Canadian dish featuring crispy fries, cheese curds, and rich gravy.

## Ingredients

### For the Fries
- 4 large russet potatoes
- Vegetable oil for frying
- Salt to taste

### For the Gravy
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 20 oz beef broth
- 10 oz chicken broth
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For Assembly
- 2 cups fresh cheese curds (at room temperature)

## Instructions

### Prepare the Fries
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into 1/4-inch thick fries
2. Rinse the cut fries in cold water to remove excess starch
3. Pat dry thoroughly with paper towels
4. Heat oil to 300°F (150°C) for the first fry
5. Fry potatoes in batches for 5-6 minutes until soft but not browned
6. Remove and let drain on paper towels
7. Increase oil temperature to 375°F (190°C)
8. Fry again for 2-3 minutes until golden and crispy
9. Season with salt immediately

### Make the Gravy
1. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat
2. Whisk in flour and cook for 2-3 minutes until golden brown
3. Gradually add beef and chicken broth while whisking
4. Add garlic powder and onion powder
5. Simmer for 10-15 minutes until thickened
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste
7. Keep warm until ready to serve

### Assemble the Poutine
1. Place hot fries in a serving dish
2. Add cheese curds on top while fries are still hot
3. Pour hot gravy generously over the fries and cheese
4. Serve immediately while the cheese is melting

## Tips
- Use fresh cheese curds for the authentic squeak!
- Make sure both fries and gravy are hot to properly melt the cheese
- Don't skimp on the gravy - poutine should be generously sauced
- Serve immediately for the best texture

## Serving Size
Serves 4 people

Enjoy your homemade poutine! 🍟🧀
