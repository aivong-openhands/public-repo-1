# Classic Tiramisu Recipe

## Ingredients:

*   6 large egg yolks
*   3/4 cup granulated sugar
*   1 3/4 pounds mascarpone cheese, at room temperature
*   1 1/2 cups strong brewed espresso, cooled
*   1/4 cup coffee liqueur (optional, e.g., Kahlúa, Marsala wine)
*   24-30 ladyfingers (savoiardi)
*   Unsweetened cocoa powder, for dusting
*   1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions:

1.  **Prepare the Sabayon (Egg Yolk Base):**
    *   In a heatproof bowl set over a saucepan of simmering water (bain-marie), whisk together the egg yolks and granulated sugar until pale yellow and thickened, about 7-10 minutes. The mixture should reach 160°F (71°C) to be safe to eat. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.

2.  **Add Mascarpone:**
    *   Once the sabayon has cooled, gently fold in the mascarpone cheese and vanilla extract until just combined and smooth. Be careful not to overmix, or the mascarpone can curdle.

3.  **Prepare the Coffee Mixture:**
    *   In a shallow dish, combine the cooled espresso and coffee liqueur (if using).

4.  **Assemble the Tiramisu:**
    *   Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the coffee mixture, one at a time, ensuring it's saturated but not soggy. Do not soak for too long.
    *   Arrange a single layer of soaked ladyfingers at the bottom of a 9x13 inch (23x33 cm) baking dish or serving platter.
    *   Spread half of the mascarpone mixture evenly over the ladyfingers.
    *   Repeat with another layer of soaked ladyfingers and the remaining mascarpone mixture.

5.  **Chill and Dust:**
    *   Cover the dish with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours, or preferably overnight, to allow the flavors to meld and the tiramisu to set.
    *   Before serving, dust generously with unsweetened cocoa powder.

## Tips:

*   Use high-quality espresso for the best flavor.
*   If you don't have ladyfingers, you can use sponge cake slices.
*   For a richer flavor, you can separate the egg whites and whip them to soft peaks, then gently fold them into the mascarpone mixture after the yolks, sugar, and mascarpone are combined.
*   Avoid over-soaking the ladyfingers, as this will make the tiramisu watery.
