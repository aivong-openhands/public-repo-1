# Chocolate Mint Cookies Recipe

A delicious recipe for soft and chewy chocolate cookies with a refreshing mint flavor.

## Ingredients

### For the Cookies:
- 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
- ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened
- ¾ cup granulated sugar
- ¾ cup packed brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon peppermint extract
- 2 cups chocolate chips

### For the Mint Glaze (Optional):
- 1½ cups powdered sugar
- 3-4 tablespoons milk
- ½ teaspoon peppermint extract
- 2-3 drops green food coloring (optional)

## Instructions

### Making the Cookies:

1. **Preheat oven** to 375°F (190°C).

2. **Mix dry ingredients**: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.

3. **Cream butter and sugars**: In a large bowl, beat softened butter with granulated sugar and brown sugar until light and fluffy (about 2-3 minutes).

4. **Add eggs and extracts**: Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla and peppermint extracts.

5. **Combine wet and dry ingredients**: Gradually blend in the flour mixture until just combined. Don't overmix.

6. **Add chocolate chips**: Fold in chocolate chips until evenly distributed.

7. **Shape cookies**: Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto ungreased cookie sheets, spacing them about 2 inches apart.

8. **Bake**: Bake for 9-11 minutes or until edges are set. Centers may look slightly underdone.

9. **Cool**: Let cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.

### Making the Mint Glaze (Optional):

1. **Mix glaze ingredients**: Whisk together powdered sugar, milk, peppermint extract, and food coloring until smooth.

2. **Apply glaze**: Once cookies are completely cool, drizzle glaze over cookies or dip cookie tops into glaze.

3. **Set glaze**: Allow glaze to set for 15-20 minutes before serving.

## Tips for Perfect Mint Cookies

- **Don't overbake**: Cookies will continue to cook slightly after removing from oven
- **Chill dough**: For thicker cookies, chill dough for 30 minutes before baking
- **Peppermint strength**: Start with less peppermint extract and add more to taste - it can be overpowering
- **Storage**: Store in airtight container for up to 1 week
- **Freezing**: Cookie dough can be frozen for up to 3 months

## Variations

- **Double Mint**: Add crushed peppermint candies or mint chocolate chips
- **White Chocolate**: Substitute white chocolate chips for regular chocolate chips
- **Sandwich Cookies**: Make sandwich cookies with mint buttercream filling

## Yield
Makes approximately 48 cookies

## Prep Time
- Preparation: 20 minutes
- Baking: 11 minutes per batch
- Total: About 45 minutes (including cooling time)
