# Classic Spaghetti with Marinara Sauce

A delicious and authentic Italian spaghetti recipe that serves 4 people.

## Ingredients

### For the Pasta:
- 1 pound (450g) spaghetti
- 4-6 quarts water
- 2 tablespoons salt
- 1 tablespoon olive oil (optional)

### For the Marinara Sauce:
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon sugar (to balance acidity)
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish

### For Serving:
- Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- Fresh basil leaves
- Extra virgin olive oil for drizzling

## Instructions

### Making the Sauce:

1. **Heat the olive oil** in a large saucepan over medium heat.

2. **Sauté the garlic** for about 1-2 minutes until fragrant, being careful not to burn it.

3. **Add the tomatoes** (crushed and diced), tomato paste, and sugar. Stir well to combine.

4. **Season** with basil, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper.

5. **Simmer** the sauce for 20-30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens slightly. Adjust seasoning to taste.

### Cooking the Pasta:

1. **Bring water to a boil** in a large pot. Add salt (the water should taste like seawater).

2. **Add the spaghetti** and stir immediately to prevent sticking.

3. **Cook** according to package directions, usually 8-10 minutes, until al dente (firm to the bite).

4. **Reserve** 1 cup of pasta cooking water before draining.

5. **Drain** the pasta (do not rinse).

### Combining:

1. **Add the drained pasta** directly to the sauce and toss to coat evenly.

2. **If needed**, add some reserved pasta water to achieve desired consistency.

3. **Cook together** for 1-2 minutes, allowing the pasta to absorb the sauce flavors.

### Serving:

1. **Plate the spaghetti** and top with freshly grated Parmesan cheese.

2. **Garnish** with fresh basil leaves.

3. **Drizzle** with a little extra virgin olive oil if desired.

4. **Serve immediately** while hot.

## Tips

- **Al dente** means the pasta should have a slight firmness when you bite into it
- **Save pasta water** - the starchy water helps the sauce adhere to the pasta
- **Don't rinse** the pasta after draining - you want the starch to help bind the sauce
- **Quality matters** - use good quality canned tomatoes (San Marzano if possible)
- **Fresh is best** - fresh basil and quality Parmesan make a big difference

## Variations

- **Add protein**: Meatballs, Italian sausage, or ground beef
- **Make it vegetarian**: Add sautéed mushrooms, zucchini, or eggplant
- **Spicy version**: Increase red pepper flakes or add fresh chili peppers
- **Creamy option**: Stir in a splash of heavy cream at the end

Enjoy your homemade spaghetti! 🍝
