# Chocolate Lava Cake Recipe

A decadent dessert with a gooey molten chocolate center that flows out when you cut into it.

## Ingredients

### For the cakes:
- 4 oz (113g) dark chocolate (70% cocoa), chopped
- 1/2 cup (113g) unsalted butter
- 2 large eggs
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- Pinch of salt
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

### For serving:
- Powdered sugar for dusting
- Fresh berries (optional)
- Vanilla ice cream (optional)

## Equipment
- 4 ramekins (6 oz each)
- Double boiler or microwave-safe bowl
- Mixing bowls
- Whisk

## Instructions

### Preparation (10 minutes)
1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Generously butter the inside of 4 ramekins and dust with cocoa powder or sugar. Tap out excess.
3. Place ramekins on a baking sheet.

### Making the batter (10 minutes)
4. Melt the chocolate and butter together in a double boiler or microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring until smooth. Let cool slightly.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until thick and pale, about 2-3 minutes.
6. Add the vanilla extract to the egg mixture.
7. Gently fold the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture.
8. Sift in the flour and salt, then fold gently until just combined. Don't overmix.

### Baking (12-14 minutes)
9. Divide the batter evenly among the prepared ramekins, filling them about 3/4 full.
10. Bake for 12-14 minutes. The edges should be firm but the center will still jiggle slightly.
11. Remove from oven and let stand for 1 minute.

### Serving
12. Run a knife around the edge of each ramekin.
13. Carefully invert each cake onto a serving plate.
14. Dust with powdered sugar.
15. Serve immediately with fresh berries and vanilla ice cream if desired.

## Tips
- **Timing is crucial**: Underbake rather than overbake for the perfect molten center.
- **Make ahead**: You can prepare the batter and fill the ramekins up to 24 hours in advance. Refrigerate and add 2-3 minutes to baking time if baking from cold.
- **Test doneness**: The edges should pull away from the ramekin slightly and the top should be set but the center still jiggles.
- **Temperature matters**: Make sure your oven is fully preheated for best results.

## Variations
- Add a teaspoon of espresso powder to the chocolate for a mocha version
- Place a piece of caramel in the center before baking for salted caramel lava cakes
- Use white chocolate instead of dark chocolate for a sweeter version
