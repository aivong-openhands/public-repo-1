# Chocolate Lava Cake Recipe

A decadent dessert with a gooey molten chocolate center that flows out when you cut into it.

## Ingredients

### For the Cakes:
- 4 oz (113g) dark chocolate (60-70% cocoa), chopped
- 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 2 large eggs
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- Pinch of salt
- Butter and cocoa powder for greasing ramekins

### For Serving (Optional):
- Vanilla ice cream
- Fresh berries
- Powdered sugar for dusting
- Whipped cream

## Equipment
- 4 ramekins (6 oz each)
- Double boiler or microwave-safe bowl
- Mixing bowls
- Whisk
- Baking sheet

## Instructions

### Preparation (10 minutes)
1. **Preheat oven** to 425°F (220°C).

2. **Prepare ramekins**: Generously butter the inside of 4 ramekins, then dust with cocoa powder. Tap out excess. Place on a baking sheet.

3. **Melt chocolate and butter**: In a double boiler or microwave, melt the chocolate and butter together, stirring until smooth. Let cool slightly (about 5 minutes).

### Making the Batter (5 minutes)
4. **Mix eggs and sugar**: In a separate bowl, whisk together eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until thick and pale yellow (about 2 minutes).

5. **Combine**: Gently fold the chocolate mixture into the egg mixture until well combined.

6. **Add flour**: Sift in the flour and salt, then fold gently until just incorporated. Don't overmix.

### Baking (12-14 minutes)
7. **Fill ramekins**: Divide the batter evenly among the prepared ramekins (they should be about 3/4 full).

8. **Bake**: Place the baking sheet in the oven and bake for 12-14 minutes. The edges should be firm, but the center will still be soft.

9. **Rest**: Remove from oven and let stand for 1 minute.

### Serving
10. **Unmold**: Run a knife around the edge of each cake to loosen. Place a plate over each ramekin and carefully invert. Gently lift off the ramekin.

11. **Garnish**: Dust with powdered sugar, add a scoop of vanilla ice cream, and fresh berries if desired.

12. **Serve immediately**: The lava center is best enjoyed warm!

## Tips for Success
- **Don't overbake**: The center should still jiggle slightly when you remove them from the oven. This ensures the molten center.
- **Make ahead**: You can prepare the batter and fill the ramekins up to 6 hours in advance. Refrigerate, then add 2 minutes to baking time.
- **Temperature matters**: Let the chocolate mixture cool slightly before adding to eggs to prevent scrambling.
- **Quality chocolate**: Use good quality chocolate for the best flavor.

## Variations
- **Espresso**: Add 1 teaspoon instant espresso powder to enhance chocolate flavor
- **Orange**: Add 1 teaspoon orange zest to the batter
- **Salted caramel**: Place a caramel square in the center of each ramekin before baking
- **White chocolate**: Substitute with white chocolate for a sweeter version

## Serving Suggestions
Pairs beautifully with:
- Vanilla ice cream or gelato
- Fresh raspberries or strawberries
- Coffee or espresso
- Dessert wine or port
