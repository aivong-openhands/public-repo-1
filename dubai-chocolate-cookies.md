# Dubai Chocolate Cookies

A decadent cookie recipe inspired by the viral Dubai chocolate, featuring a rich chocolate cookie base filled with a luscious pistachio-tahini filling and crispy kadayif.

## Ingredients

### For the Chocolate Cookie Dough:
- 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup (50g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup (170g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup (200g) brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup (175g) dark chocolate chips

### For the Dubai Filling:
- 1/2 cup (125g) tahini
- 1/4 cup (60g) unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup (100g) powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup (60g) crushed pistachios
- 1 cup (50g) kadayif (shredded phyllo dough), toasted
- 2 tbsp honey
- Pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp cardamom (optional)

### For Topping:
- Extra crushed pistachios
- Flaky sea salt
- Melted dark chocolate for drizzling

## Instructions

### Prepare the Dubai Filling:
1. Toast the kadayif in a dry pan over medium heat until golden and crispy, about 3-4 minutes. Set aside to cool.
2. In a bowl, mix together tahini, softened butter, powdered sugar, and honey until smooth and creamy.
3. Fold in the crushed pistachios, toasted kadayif, cardamom (if using), and a pinch of salt.
4. Refrigerate the filling while you prepare the cookie dough.

### Make the Cookie Dough:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. In a large bowl, cream together softened butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy (about 3 minutes).
4. Beat in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla extract.
5. Gradually mix in the dry ingredients until just combined.
6. Fold in chocolate chips.

### Assemble the Cookies:
1. Scoop about 2 tablespoons of dough and flatten it in your palm.
2. Place a teaspoon of the Dubai filling in the center.
3. Take another tablespoon of dough, flatten it, and place it on top of the filling.
4. Seal the edges and roll into a ball, ensuring the filling is completely enclosed.
5. Place cookies on the prepared baking sheet, spacing them about 2 inches apart.

### Bake:
1. Bake for 12-14 minutes, until the edges are set but the centers are still slightly soft.
2. Remove from oven and let cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes.
3. Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

### Finishing Touches:
1. Once cooled, drizzle melted dark chocolate over the cookies.
2. Sprinkle with extra crushed pistachios and flaky sea salt.
3. Let the chocolate set before serving.

## Tips:
- Make sure the filling is completely enclosed to prevent leaking during baking.
- If you can't find kadayif, you can substitute with toasted shredded phyllo dough or finely crushed cornflakes for crunch.
- Store cookies in an airtight container for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
- For an extra indulgent touch, sandwich two cookies with additional Dubai filling.

## Yield:
Makes approximately 18-20 large stuffed cookies

## Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 14 minutes | Total Time: 44 minutes

Enjoy these luxurious cookies that bring the viral Dubai chocolate trend to your home kitchen!
