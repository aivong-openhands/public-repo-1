# Classic Pour Over Coffee Recipe

## Ingredients:
- 20g (about 3 tablespoons) freshly ground coffee beans (medium-coarse grind)
- 320ml (about 11 oz) filtered water, heated to 195-205°F (90-96°C)

## Equipment:
- Pour over dripper (V60, Chemex, or similar)
- Paper filter
- Coffee grinder
- Kettle (gooseneck preferred)
- Scale (optional but recommended)
- Timer

## Instructions:

1. **Prep the filter**: Place the paper filter in your dripper and rinse with hot water to remove paper taste and preheat the vessel. Discard the rinse water.

2. **Add coffee**: Add the ground coffee to the filter and create a small well in the center.

3. **Bloom (0:00-0:45)**: Pour about 40-50ml of hot water over the grounds, ensuring all coffee is saturated. Let it bloom for 30-45 seconds - you'll see bubbles as CO2 releases.

4. **First pour (0:45-1:15)**: Slowly pour water in a circular motion from the center outward, reaching about 150ml total.

5. **Second pour (1:15-2:30)**: Continue pouring in circles until you reach 320ml total. Maintain a steady, controlled pour.

6. **Drawdown (2:30-3:30)**: Allow all water to drip through. Total brew time should be 3-4 minutes.

7. **Serve**: Remove the dripper, swirl the coffee gently, and enjoy!

## Tips:
- Use freshly roasted beans (within 2-4 weeks of roast date)
- Grind just before brewing for best flavor
- Experiment with the coffee-to-water ratio (1:16 is a good starting point)
- Water quality matters - use filtered water if possible

## Flavor Profile:
Clean, bright, and complex with clarity of flavor notes.

## Variations:
- **Stronger brew**: Use a 1:15 ratio (21g coffee to 320ml water)
- **Lighter brew**: Use a 1:17 ratio (19g coffee to 320ml water)
- **Iced pour over**: Brew over ice using half the water amount, replace other half with ice
