# Classic Homemade Lemonade

A refreshing and easy-to-make lemonade recipe perfect for hot summer days!

## Ingredients

- **1 cup fresh lemon juice** (about 4-6 lemons)
- **1 cup granulated sugar**
- **6 cups cold water** (divided)
- **Ice cubes**
- **Lemon slices** (for garnish, optional)
- **Fresh mint leaves** (for garnish, optional)

## Instructions

### 1. Make Simple Syrup
- In a small saucepan, combine 1 cup of water and 1 cup of sugar
- Heat over medium heat, stirring until sugar completely dissolves
- Remove from heat and let cool (this is your simple syrup)

### 2. Juice the Lemons
- Roll lemons on the counter with your palm to make them easier to juice
- Cut and squeeze lemons until you have 1 cup of fresh juice
- Strain out seeds if needed

### 3. Combine
- In a large pitcher, mix the lemon juice and simple syrup
- Add the remaining 5 cups of cold water
- Stir well to combine

### 4. Chill & Serve
- Refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or serve immediately over ice
- Garnish with lemon slices and fresh mint if desired

## Tips

- **Adjust sweetness:** Taste and add more water if too sweet, or more simple syrup if too tart
- **Quick version:** Skip the simple syrup and just stir sugar directly into the mixture (works better with warm water)
- **Variations:** Add strawberries, raspberries, or herbs like basil for a twist

## Yield

About 8 cups (serves 6-8)
