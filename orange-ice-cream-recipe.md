# Orange Ice Cream Recipe

A delicious and refreshing homemade orange ice cream with a creamy texture and bright citrus flavor.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 cup whole milk
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon orange zest (from 1-2 oranges)
- 3/4 cup freshly squeezed orange juice (from 2-3 oranges)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 5 large egg yolks

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Base
- In a medium saucepan, combine the heavy cream, milk, 1/2 cup of sugar, salt, and orange zest
- Heat over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the mixture is warm and the sugar dissolves (do not boil)

### 2. Temper the Egg Yolks
- In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg yolks and remaining 1/4 cup sugar until pale and thick
- Slowly pour about 1 cup of the warm cream mixture into the egg yolks while whisking constantly
- This tempers the eggs and prevents them from scrambling

### 3. Cook the Custard
- Pour the egg mixture back into the saucepan with the remaining cream mixture
- Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon or spatula
- Continue cooking until the mixture thickens and coats the back of the spoon (about 170-175°F)
- Do not let it boil

### 4. Add Orange Flavor
- Remove from heat and stir in the freshly squeezed orange juice and vanilla extract
- Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean bowl to remove the zest and any cooked egg bits

### 5. Chill
- Cover the bowl with plastic wrap, pressing it directly onto the surface to prevent a skin from forming
- Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight until completely cold

### 6. Churn
- Pour the chilled mixture into an ice cream maker
- Churn according to your ice cream maker's instructions (usually 20-25 minutes)
- The ice cream should be thick and creamy, like soft-serve

### 7. Freeze
- Transfer the ice cream to a freezer-safe container
- Press parchment paper or plastic wrap directly on the surface
- Freeze for at least 4 hours or until firm

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve in waffle cones or bowls
- Garnish with candied orange peel
- Pair with chocolate sauce or fresh berries
- Top with whipped cream and a sprig of mint

## Storage

Store in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 2 weeks. Let soften at room temperature for 5-10 minutes before scooping for easier serving.

## Tips

- Use fresh oranges for the best flavor - avoid bottled juice
- Don't skip straining the custard; it ensures a smooth texture
- For a more intense orange flavor, add 1/2 teaspoon of orange extract
- For a creamier texture, you can use 2 1/2 cups heavy cream and 1/2 cup milk instead

Enjoy your homemade orange ice cream! 🍊🍦
