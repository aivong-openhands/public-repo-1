# White Chocolate Latte Recipe

A delicious and creamy white chocolate latte that's perfect for any time of day!

## Ingredients

- 2 shots of espresso (about 2 oz)
- 2 tablespoons white chocolate chips or white chocolate sauce
- 8 oz steamed milk (whole milk recommended for creaminess)
- Whipped cream (optional, for topping)
- White chocolate shavings (optional, for garnish)

## Equipment

- Espresso machine or moka pot
- Milk frother or steam wand
- Coffee mug (12 oz capacity)
- Small saucepan (if melting chocolate chips)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the White Chocolate
1. If using white chocolate chips, melt them gently:
   - Place chips in a small bowl
   - Microwave in 15-second intervals, stirring between each, until smooth
   - Alternatively, melt in a small saucepan over low heat
2. If using white chocolate sauce, have it ready to pour

### Step 2: Brew the Espresso
1. Pull 2 shots of espresso directly into your mug
2. Immediately add the melted white chocolate or sauce to the hot espresso
3. Stir vigorously until the chocolate is completely dissolved and well combined

### Step 3: Steam the Milk
1. Steam your milk to approximately 150-160°F (65-70°C)
2. Create a smooth, velvety microfoam by positioning the steam wand just below the milk's surface
3. Swirl the milk pitcher to incorporate the foam evenly

### Step 4: Combine and Serve
1. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso and white chocolate mixture
2. Hold back the foam with a spoon, then spoon the foam on top
3. Optional: Top with whipped cream and white chocolate shavings

### Step 5: Enjoy!
Sip and savor your homemade white chocolate latte!

## Tips & Variations

- **Iced Version**: Pour the espresso and white chocolate mixture over ice, then add cold milk
- **Extra Sweet**: Add 1-2 teaspoons of vanilla syrup for added sweetness
- **Mocha Twist**: Add a small amount of cocoa powder for a marbled effect
- **Dairy-Free**: Use oat milk or almond milk as alternatives
- **Spice It Up**: Add a pinch of cinnamon or cardamom for warmth
