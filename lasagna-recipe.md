# Classic Italian Lasagna

## Ingredients

### For the Meat Sauce:
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lb (450g) ground beef
- 1 lb (450g) Italian sausage
- 2 cans (28 oz each) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 2 tsp dried basil
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 2 bay leaves

### For the Cheese Filling:
- 32 oz (900g) ricotta cheese
- 2 large eggs
- ½ cup fresh parsley, chopped
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

### Assembly:
- 1 lb (450g) lasagna noodles
- 4 cups (1 lb) shredded mozzarella cheese
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Meat Sauce (1 hour):
- Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat
- Sauté onion until soft (5 minutes), add garlic and cook 1 minute more
- Add ground beef and sausage, breaking up with a spoon, cook until browned
- Drain excess fat
- Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, herbs, salt, pepper, and bay leaves
- Simmer uncovered for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally
- Remove bay leaves

### 2. Prepare the Cheese Filling:
- Mix ricotta, eggs, parsley, Parmesan, salt, and pepper in a bowl
- Set aside

### 3. Cook the Noodles:
- Boil lasagna noodles according to package directions
- Drain and lay flat on parchment paper to prevent sticking

### 4. Assemble (in a 9x13 inch baking dish):
- Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
- Spread 1½ cups meat sauce on bottom of dish
- Layer 4-5 noodles (slightly overlapping)
- Spread ⅓ of ricotta mixture
- Sprinkle 1 cup mozzarella
- Repeat layers twice more: sauce, noodles, ricotta, mozzarella
- Top layer: noodles, remaining sauce, remaining mozzarella, Parmesan

### 5. Bake:
- Cover with aluminum foil (spray underside with cooking spray to prevent sticking)
- Bake 25 minutes covered
- Remove foil, bake 25 minutes more until bubbly and golden
- Let rest 15-20 minutes before cutting

## Serves: 10-12 people

## Tips:
- Make ahead: Assemble and refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking
- Freezer-friendly: Freeze unbaked (up to 3 months), add 30 minutes to baking time
- For no-boil noodles: Add extra sauce and increase covered baking time by 10 minutes

Enjoy your homemade lasagna! 🍝
