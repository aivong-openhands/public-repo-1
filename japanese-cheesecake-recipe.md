# Japanese Cheesecake (Cotton Cheesecake)

A light, fluffy, and jiggly cheesecake with a delicate texture that melts in your mouth.

## Ingredients

### For the Cake
- 250g (8.8 oz) cream cheese, softened
- 50g (3.5 tbsp) unsalted butter
- 100ml (1/3 cup + 1 tbsp) whole milk
- 6 large eggs, separated
- 140g (2/3 cup) granulated sugar
- 60g (1/2 cup) cake flour
- 20g (3 tbsp) cornstarch
- 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Pinch of salt

### For the Pan
- Butter for greasing
- Parchment paper

## Equipment
- 8-inch (20cm) round cake pan
- Large roasting pan (for water bath)
- Electric mixer
- Double boiler or microwave-safe bowl
- Sieve

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. Preheat oven to 320°F (160°C).
2. Grease the cake pan and line the bottom and sides with parchment paper. The paper should extend above the rim by 2 inches.
3. Prepare a water bath by placing a roasting pan in the oven and filling it with hot water (about 1 inch deep).

### Making the Batter
1. **Melt the cream cheese mixture**: In a double boiler or microwave, melt cream cheese, butter, and milk together until smooth. Stir well and let cool slightly.

2. **Add egg yolks**: Add egg yolks one at a time to the cream cheese mixture, whisking after each addition. Add vanilla extract and lemon juice. Mix until smooth.

3. **Sift dry ingredients**: Sift together cake flour and cornstarch. Add to the cream cheese mixture and whisk until just combined and smooth.

4. **Beat egg whites**: In a clean, dry bowl, beat egg whites with salt until foamy. Add cream of tartar and continue beating. Gradually add sugar and beat until soft peaks form (not stiff peaks - this is important!).

5. **Fold together**: Gently fold 1/3 of the meringue into the cream cheese mixture to lighten it. Then fold in the remaining meringue in two additions, being careful not to deflate the batter.

### Baking
1. Pour batter into the prepared pan and tap gently on the counter to release air bubbles.

2. Place the cake pan in the water bath in the oven.

3. Bake at 320°F (160°C) for 70-75 minutes, until the top is golden brown and the cake is set but still jiggly in the center.

4. Turn off the oven and leave the cake inside with the door slightly ajar for 30 minutes.

5. Remove from oven and let cool to room temperature in the pan.

6. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight before serving.

## Tips for Success

- **Room temperature ingredients**: Make sure cream cheese and eggs are at room temperature for better mixing.

- **Soft peaks only**: Over-beating the meringue will create a denser cake. Stop at soft peaks.

- **Gentle folding**: Fold the meringue carefully to maintain the airy texture.

- **Water bath is essential**: This creates a humid environment and prevents cracking.

- **Don't open the oven**: Avoid opening the oven door during baking as temperature changes can cause the cake to deflate.

- **The jiggle is good**: The cake should still jiggle slightly when done - it will set as it cools.

- **Prevent cracking**: If cracks appear, the oven temperature may be too high or the meringue was over-beaten.

## Serving

Serve chilled, dusted with powdered sugar. The cake is best enjoyed within 2-3 days and should be stored in the refrigerator.

## Notes

This cheesecake has a delicate, cotton-like texture that's characteristic of Japanese style. It's less sweet and much lighter than traditional Western cheesecakes. The cake will naturally deflate slightly as it cools - this is normal!

Enjoy your fluffy, jiggly Japanese cheesecake! ✨
