# Classic Flan Recipe

## Ingredients:

*   1 cup granulated sugar
*   1/4 cup water
*   1 (12 ounce) can evaporated milk
*   1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
*   4 large eggs
*   1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions:

1.  **Make the caramel:** In a heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat, combine the sugar and water. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the sugar dissolves and the mixture turns a deep amber color. Be careful not to burn it.
2.  Immediately pour the hot caramel into a 9-inch round baking dish or 6-8 ramekins, tilting to coat the bottom and sides evenly. Set aside.
3.  **Make the custard:** In a large bowl, whisk together the evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk, eggs, and vanilla extract until well combined and smooth.
4.  Pour the custard mixture over the cooled caramel in the baking dish or ramekins.
5.  **Bake the flan:** Place the baking dish or ramekins in a larger baking pan. Fill the larger pan with hot water to reach about halfway up the sides of the flan dish(es). This creates a water bath, which helps the flan cook evenly.
6.  Bake in a preheated oven at 350°F (175°C) for 50-60 minutes, or until the edges are set but the center still jiggles slightly.
7.  Carefully remove the flan from the water bath and let it cool completely on a wire rack.
8.  **Chill and serve:** Once cooled, cover the flan and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight, to allow it to set fully.
9.  To serve, run a thin knife around the edge of the dish(es) and invert onto a serving plate. The caramel sauce will pour over the flan.

Enjoy your delicious homemade flan! Enjoy!
