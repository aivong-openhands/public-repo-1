# Flan Recipe

## Ingredients

* 1 cup sugar
* 1/4 cup water
* 3 eggs
* 1 can (14 ounces) sweetened condensed milk
* 1 can (12 fluid ounces) evaporated milk
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
2. In a small saucepan over medium-low heat, combine sugar and water. Cook without stirring until sugar is dissolved and mixture turns a deep golden caramel color. Carefully pour caramel into a 9-inch round baking dish, tilting to coat the bottom evenly. Set aside.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together eggs, sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk, and vanilla extract until smooth. Pour mixture over the caramel in the baking dish.
4. Place the baking dish in a larger baking pan. Fill the larger pan with hot water to reach halfway up the sides of the baking dish (this is a water bath or "bain-marie").
5. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until the center is just set. A knife inserted near the center should come out clean.
6. Carefully remove from the water bath and let cool completely on a wire rack. Once cool, refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight.
7. To serve, run a knife around the edge of the dish, then invert onto a serving plate. The caramel will run over the flan.
