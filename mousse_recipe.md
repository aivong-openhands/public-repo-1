# Chocolate Mousse Recipe

## Ingredients:

*   1 cup heavy cream
*   1/2 cup powdered sugar
*   1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
*   1 teaspoon vanilla extract
*   Pinch of salt

## Instructions:

1.  In a large bowl, combine heavy cream, powdered sugar, cocoa powder, vanilla extract, and salt.
2.  Using an electric mixer, beat on medium-high speed until stiff peaks form. Be careful not to overbeat, or it will turn grainy.
3.  Spoon the mousse into individual serving dishes.
4.  Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
5.  Garnish with chocolate shavings or fresh berries if desired.
