# Macchiato Recipe

A classic Italian espresso macchiato - a shot of espresso "marked" with a dollop of foamed milk.

## Ingredients

- 18-20g freshly ground espresso coffee beans
- 1-2 tablespoons of fresh whole milk

## Equipment

- Espresso machine
- Coffee grinder
- Espresso cup (2-3 oz / 60-90 ml)
- Milk pitcher

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Espresso**
   - Grind 18-20g of fresh coffee beans to a fine espresso grind
   - Distribute the grounds evenly in the portafilter
   - Tamp firmly and evenly with about 30 pounds of pressure
   - Lock the portafilter into the espresso machine

2. **Pull the Shot**
   - Start the extraction
   - Pull a single or double shot of espresso (1-2 oz / 30-60 ml)
   - Extraction time should be 25-30 seconds
   - The espresso should have a rich golden-brown crema on top

3. **Steam the Milk**
   - Pour cold milk into a small pitcher
   - Steam the milk to create microfoam (not too much foam, just enough to "mark" the espresso)
   - The milk should be heated to about 150-155°F (65-68°C)

4. **Assemble the Macchiato**
   - Pour the espresso into a demitasse or small espresso cup
   - Add a small dollop (about 1 tablespoon) of foamed milk on top
   - The milk should just "mark" or "stain" the espresso surface

5. **Serve Immediately**
   - Serve right away while hot
   - Enjoy the perfect balance of bold espresso with a touch of creamy milk

## Tips

- Use freshly roasted coffee beans for the best flavor
- The milk should complement, not overpower, the espresso
- For a traditional macchiato, use minimal milk - just enough to create a small white mark on the dark espresso
- Serve in a preheated cup to maintain temperature

## Variations

- **Latte Macchiato**: Reverse the proportions - start with steamed milk and "mark" it with espresso
- **Caramel Macchiato**: Add vanilla syrup and caramel drizzle (popular at coffee chains but not traditional)

---

*Macchiato* means "marked" or "stained" in Italian, referring to the small mark of milk on the espresso.
