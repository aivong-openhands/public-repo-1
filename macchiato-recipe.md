# Macchiato Recipe

A classic Italian espresso macchiato - a perfect balance of bold espresso and a touch of creamy milk foam.

## Ingredients

- 1 shot (30ml) of freshly brewed espresso
- 1-2 tablespoons of steamed milk foam

## Equipment Needed

- Espresso machine
- Small espresso cup (demitasse)
- Milk steaming pitcher

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the espresso**
   - Grind fresh coffee beans to a fine consistency
   - Tamp the grounds firmly and evenly in the portafilter
   - Pull a single shot of espresso (about 30ml) into a demitasse cup

2. **Steam the milk**
   - Pour cold milk into the steaming pitcher (you only need a small amount)
   - Steam the milk until it reaches about 60-65°C (140-150°F)
   - Create a microfoam texture by positioning the steam wand just below the surface

3. **Assemble the macchiato**
   - Using a spoon, add 1-2 tablespoons of milk foam on top of the espresso
   - The foam should "mark" (macchiare in Italian) the espresso, hence the name

4. **Serve immediately**
   - Enjoy your macchiato while hot

## Tips

- Use high-quality, freshly roasted espresso beans for the best flavor
- The traditional macchiato uses very little milk - just enough to "stain" the espresso
- For a sweeter variation, you can add a small amount of vanilla or caramel syrup
- The ratio should be about 90% espresso to 10% milk foam

## Variations

- **Caramel Macchiato**: Add caramel syrup to the espresso and drizzle caramel on top of the foam
- **Iced Macchiato**: Pour espresso over ice and top with cold milk foam
- **Latte Macchiato**: Reverse the traditional recipe by pouring espresso over steamed milk

Enjoy your perfectly crafted macchiato! ☕
