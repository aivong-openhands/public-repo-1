# Classic Latte Recipe

A delicious and creamy latte made with espresso and steamed milk.

## Ingredients

- 2 shots of espresso (about 2 oz / 60 ml)
- 8-10 oz (240-300 ml) of whole milk
- Optional: sugar or sweetener to taste
- Optional: flavored syrup (vanilla, caramel, etc.)

## Equipment

- Espresso machine
- Milk steamer or frother
- 12 oz latte cup

## Instructions

1. **Brew the espresso**: Pull 2 shots of espresso (about 2 oz) using your espresso machine. Pour directly into your latte cup.

2. **Steam the milk**: Pour cold milk into a steaming pitcher. Steam the milk to about 150-155°F (65-68°C), creating a smooth microfoam with a glossy texture.

3. **Combine**: Pour the steamed milk over the espresso in a steady stream, holding back the foam with a spoon initially, then spooning the foam on top.

4. **Optional latte art**: For latte art, pour the milk from a height at first to mix, then bring the pitcher closer to the surface and pour steadily in the center to create designs.

5. **Serve immediately**: Enjoy your freshly made latte while it's hot!

## Tips

- Use freshly ground coffee beans for the best espresso
- Whole milk creates the creamiest foam, but you can use any milk alternative
- Practice your milk steaming technique for the perfect microfoam
- The ideal ratio is about 1 part espresso to 3-4 parts steamed milk

## Variations

- **Vanilla Latte**: Add 1-2 pumps of vanilla syrup
- **Caramel Latte**: Add caramel syrup and top with caramel drizzle
- **Iced Latte**: Pour espresso and cold milk over ice
- **Mocha Latte**: Add chocolate syrup or cocoa powder

Enjoy your perfect homemade latte! ☕
