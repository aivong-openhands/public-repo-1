# Matcha Latte Recipe

A delicious and creamy matcha latte that you can make at home!

## Ingredients

- 1-2 teaspoons matcha powder (ceremonial grade recommended)
- 2 tablespoons hot water (not boiling, around 175°F/80°C)
- 1 cup milk of choice (dairy, oat, almond, or soy)
- 1-2 teaspoons sweetener (honey, maple syrup, or sugar - optional)
- Ice (optional, for iced version)

## Equipment

- Matcha whisk (chasen) or small whisk/frother
- Small bowl or cup
- Milk frother or small saucepan
- Serving glass or mug

## Instructions

### For Hot Matcha Latte

1. **Sift the matcha powder** into a small bowl to remove any clumps.

2. **Add hot water** (about 2 tablespoons) to the matcha powder.

3. **Whisk vigorously** in a zigzag motion until the matcha is fully dissolved and slightly frothy. If using a chasen, whisk in a "W" or "M" pattern.

4. **Heat and froth the milk** using a milk frother or by heating it in a saucepan and whisking until foamy.

5. **Add sweetener** to the milk if desired, and stir to combine.

6. **Pour the frothed milk** into your serving mug.

7. **Add the whisked matcha** on top of the milk.

8. **Stir gently** and enjoy!

### For Iced Matcha Latte

1. Follow steps 1-3 above to prepare the matcha.

2. **Fill a glass with ice**.

3. **Pour cold milk** over the ice.

4. **Add sweetener** if desired and stir.

5. **Pour the whisked matcha** over the milk and ice.

6. **Stir well** and enjoy!

## Tips

- Use ceremonial grade matcha for the best flavor and smoothest texture
- Don't use boiling water as it can make the matcha bitter
- Adjust the amount of matcha powder based on your preference for strength
- For a creamier latte, use whole milk or oat milk
- Sifting the matcha powder is important to prevent clumps

## Variations

- **Vanilla Matcha Latte**: Add 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- **Honey Matcha Latte**: Sweeten with honey for a floral note
- **Coconut Matcha Latte**: Use coconut milk and add a pinch of coconut sugar

Enjoy your homemade matcha latte! ☕🍵
