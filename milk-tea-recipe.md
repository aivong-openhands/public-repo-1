# Milk Tea Recipe

A classic and comforting milk tea that you can make at home - perfect for any time of day!

## Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 2-3 black tea bags (or 2 tablespoons loose black tea)
- 1/2 cup milk of choice (dairy, oat, almond, or soy)
- 2-3 teaspoons sweetener (sugar, honey, or condensed milk)
- Ice (optional, for iced version)

## Equipment

- Small saucepan or kettle
- Tea strainer (if using loose tea)
- Spoon for stirring
- Serving mug or glass

## Instructions

### For Hot Milk Tea

1. **Boil the water** in a saucepan or kettle until it reaches a rolling boil.

2. **Add the tea bags or loose tea** to the boiling water.

3. **Steep for 3-5 minutes** depending on desired strength. For a stronger tea flavor, steep longer.

4. **Remove the tea bags** or strain out the loose tea leaves.

5. **Add the milk** to the hot tea and stir well.

6. **Add sweetener** to taste and stir until fully dissolved.

7. **Pour into your serving mug** and enjoy hot!

### For Iced Milk Tea

1. Follow steps 1-4 above to prepare the tea, but use only 1 cup of water for a more concentrated brew.

2. **Let the tea cool** for a few minutes, or place in the refrigerator to speed up cooling.

3. **Fill a glass with ice**.

4. **Pour the cooled tea** over the ice.

5. **Add the milk** and sweetener to taste.

6. **Stir well** and enjoy chilled!

## Tips

- Use strong black tea varieties like Assam, Ceylon, or English Breakfast for the best flavor
- For a creamier milk tea, increase the milk-to-tea ratio
- Adjust steeping time based on how strong you like your tea
- For extra richness, use condensed milk as both sweetener and milk
- If making iced milk tea, brew the tea stronger to account for ice dilution

## Variations

- **Hong Kong-Style Milk Tea**: Use a blend of several black teas and evaporated milk, steep multiple times for extra strength
- **Thai Milk Tea**: Add star anise and cardamom while brewing, use condensed milk and evaporated milk for sweetness
- **Bubble Milk Tea (Boba)**: Add tapioca pearls to iced milk tea for a fun, chewy texture
- **Brown Sugar Milk Tea**: Replace regular sugar with brown sugar syrup for a caramel-like flavor
- **Masala Chai**: Add spices like cardamom, cinnamon, ginger, and cloves while brewing

Enjoy your homemade milk tea! ☕🥛
