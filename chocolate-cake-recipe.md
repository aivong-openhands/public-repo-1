# Classic Chocolate Cake Recipe

A rich and moist chocolate cake that's perfect for any celebration!

## Ingredients

### For the Cake:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1½ teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup whole milk
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 cup boiling water

### For the Chocolate Frosting:
- ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
- ⅔ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 3 cups powdered sugar
- ⅓ cup whole milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

### Making the Cake:

1. **Preheat** your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.

2. **Combine dry ingredients**: In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.

3. **Mix wet ingredients**: Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla to the flour mixture. Beat on medium speed for 2 minutes.

4. **Add boiling water**: Stir in the boiling water. The batter will be thin - this is normal!

5. **Bake**: Pour batter evenly into prepared pans. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

6. **Cool**: Let cakes cool in pans for 10 minutes, then remove from pans and cool completely on wire racks.

### Making the Frosting:

1. **Cream butter**: Beat the softened butter until light and fluffy.

2. **Add cocoa**: Mix in the cocoa powder until well combined.

3. **Add sugar gradually**: Alternately add powdered sugar and milk, beating on low speed until combined.

4. **Finish**: Stir in vanilla extract. Beat on high speed for about 3 minutes until light and fluffy.

### Assembly:

1. Place one cake layer on a serving plate.
2. Spread frosting on top.
3. Add the second layer and frost the top and sides.
4. Decorate as desired!

## Tips

- Make sure all ingredients are at room temperature for best results
- Don't overmix the batter once you add the boiling water
- For extra moisture, brush layers with simple syrup before frosting
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with:
- Fresh berries
- Vanilla ice cream
- Whipped cream
- A glass of cold milk

Enjoy your delicious homemade chocolate cake! 🍰
