# Classic Chocolate Cake Recipe

A rich and moist chocolate cake that's perfect for any celebration!

## Ingredients

### For the Cake:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup strong brewed coffee (cooled)
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

### For the Chocolate Frosting:
- 1 cup unsalted butter (softened)
- 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

### Preparing the Cake:

1. **Preheat** your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.

2. **Mix dry ingredients**: In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.

3. **Combine wet ingredients**: In another bowl, beat eggs, then add coffee, buttermilk, oil, and vanilla extract. Mix well.

4. **Combine**: Gradually add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients, mixing until just combined. Don't overmix!

5. **Bake**: Divide batter evenly between the prepared pans. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

6. **Cool**: Let cakes cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks to cool completely.

### Making the Frosting:

1. **Beat butter**: In a large bowl, beat softened butter until creamy and smooth.

2. **Add dry ingredients**: Gradually add powdered sugar and cocoa powder, beating well after each addition.

3. **Add liquid**: Pour in heavy cream and vanilla extract. Beat on high speed for 3-4 minutes until light and fluffy.

4. **Season**: Add a pinch of salt to enhance the chocolate flavor.

### Assembly:

1. Place one cake layer on a serving plate.
2. Spread a generous layer of frosting on top.
3. Place the second cake layer on top.
4. Frost the top and sides of the cake with the remaining frosting.
5. Optional: Decorate with chocolate shavings or sprinkles.

## Tips

- Make sure all ingredients are at room temperature for best results
- The coffee enhances the chocolate flavor - you won't taste it in the final cake
- For extra moisture, brush each layer with simple syrup before frosting
- This cake tastes even better the next day!

## Serving

Serves: 12-16 slices
Prep Time: 20 minutes
Baking Time: 35 minutes
Total Time: 1 hour (plus cooling time)

Enjoy your delicious homemade chocolate cake! 🍰
