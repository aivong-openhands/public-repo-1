# Classic Ratatouille Recipe

A traditional French Provençal stewed vegetable dish that's both delicious and beautiful.

## Ingredients

### Main Vegetables
- 2 medium eggplants (about 1 lb)
- 4 medium zucchini (about 1 lb)
- 2 red bell peppers
- 2 yellow bell peppers
- 6 large tomatoes (or 1 can of whole tomatoes, 28 oz)
- 1 large onion, diced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced

### For Cooking
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 cup vegetable or chicken broth
- Fresh herbs: thyme, rosemary, and bay leaves
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish

## Instructions

### Preparation
1. **Prepare the vegetables**: Wash all vegetables. Cut eggplants and zucchini into 1/4-inch thick rounds. Core and dice bell peppers. Slice tomatoes into rounds.

2. **Salt the eggplant**: Place eggplant slices in a colander, sprinkle with salt, and let sit for 20 minutes to draw out moisture. Pat dry with paper towels.

### Making the Sauce
3. **Cook the base**: In a large oven-safe dish, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium heat. Sauté the diced onion until translucent (about 5 minutes).

4. **Add garlic and tomato paste**: Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes.

5. **Build the sauce**: Add diced bell peppers, broth, and half of the fresh herbs. Season with salt and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes until slightly thickened.

### Assembling the Ratatouille
6. **Arrange vegetables**: Starting from the outer edge, arrange alternating slices of eggplant, zucchini, and tomato in a spiral pattern, slightly overlapping each slice. Continue until the entire dish is filled.

7. **Season**: Drizzle the remaining olive oil over the vegetables. Sprinkle with salt, pepper, and the remaining herbs.

8. **Cover and bake**: Cover the dish with parchment paper or aluminum foil. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 40 minutes.

9. **Finish uncovered**: Remove the cover and bake for an additional 20 minutes until vegetables are tender and slightly caramelized.

### Serving
10. **Garnish and serve**: Let cool for 10 minutes. Garnish with fresh basil leaves. Serve hot or at room temperature.

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve as a main course with crusty bread
- Pair with rice, quinoa, or pasta
- Serve alongside grilled chicken or fish
- Enjoy with a glass of Provence rosé or light red wine

## Tips

- **Make ahead**: Ratatouille tastes even better the next day as flavors meld together
- **Variations**: Add mushrooms, squash, or other seasonal vegetables
- **Storage**: Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 4 days
- **Freezing**: Can be frozen for up to 3 months
