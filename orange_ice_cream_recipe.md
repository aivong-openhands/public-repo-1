# Orange Ice Cream Recipe

## Ingredients
* 2 cups heavy cream
* 1 cup whole milk
* 3/4 cup granulated sugar
* 1 tablespoon orange zest (from about 1 large orange)
* 1/2 cup fresh orange juice (from about 1 large orange)
* 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
* Pinch of salt

## Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the heavy cream, whole milk, and granulated sugar. Heat over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the sugar is fully dissolved. Do not boil.
2. Remove from heat and stir in the orange zest, orange juice, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt.
3. Pour the mixture into a bowl, cover, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight, until thoroughly chilled.
4. Once chilled, pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's instructions.
5. Transfer the churned ice cream to an airtight container and freeze for at least 2-4 hours, or until firm, before serving. Enjoy!
