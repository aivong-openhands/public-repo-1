# Tiramisu Latte Recipe

A delicious coffee drink inspired by the classic Italian dessert, combining the rich flavors of espresso, cocoa, and mascarpone.

## Ingredients

### For the latte base:
- 2 shots espresso (2 oz / 60 ml)
- 1 cup (240 ml) steamed milk
- 1 tablespoon mascarpone cheese, softened
- 1 tablespoon simple syrup or sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

### For topping:
- Whipped cream (optional)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder
- Dark chocolate shavings (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the mascarpone mixture:**
   - In a small bowl, whisk together the softened mascarpone cheese, simple syrup, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt until smooth and creamy.

2. **Brew the espresso:**
   - Pull 2 shots of espresso into your serving cup.

3. **Combine mascarpone with espresso:**
   - Add the mascarpone mixture to the hot espresso and stir vigorously until well incorporated and creamy.

4. **Steam the milk:**
   - Steam your milk to about 150-160°F (65-70°C) until it's smooth and velvety with microfoam.

5. **Pour and combine:**
   - Gently pour the steamed milk over the espresso-mascarpone mixture.
   - Stir gently to combine all ingredients.

6. **Top and garnish:**
   - Top with whipped cream if desired.
   - Dust generously with unsweetened cocoa powder using a fine mesh strainer.
   - Add dark chocolate shavings for extra indulgence.

7. **Serve immediately** while hot and enjoy your homemade tiramisu latte!

## Tips

- **Temperature matters:** Make sure the mascarpone is at room temperature for easier mixing.
- **Sweetness adjustment:** Adjust the simple syrup to your taste preference.
- **Make it iced:** For an iced version, mix all ingredients except milk, pour over ice, and add cold milk.
- **Alcohol option:** Add 1/2 oz of coffee liqueur (like Kahlúa) or amaretto for an adult version.
- **Presentation:** Use a clear glass to show off the beautiful layers.
