# Tiramisu Cheesecake Recipe

An elegant fusion of Italian tiramisu and New York-style cheesecake, featuring layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers, rich mascarpone cheesecake, and a dusting of cocoa powder.

## Prep Time
- **Prep:** 40 minutes
- **Bake:** 1 hour 15 minutes
- **Chill:** 6-8 hours (or overnight)
- **Total:** 8+ hours
- **Servings:** 12-14 slices

## Ingredients

### For the Crust:
- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs (about 16 crackers)
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

### For the Coffee Syrup:
- 1 cup strong brewed espresso or coffee, cooled
- 3 tablespoons coffee liqueur (Kahlúa or Tia Maria) - optional
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar

### For the Ladyfinger Layer:
- 24-30 ladyfinger cookies (savoiardi)

### For the Cheesecake Filling:
- 24 oz (3 packages) cream cheese, softened to room temperature
- 8 oz (1 cup) mascarpone cheese, room temperature
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon instant espresso powder
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/2 cup sour cream, room temperature

### For the Topping:
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- Dark chocolate shavings (optional)
- Whipped cream (optional)

## Instructions

### Prepare the Pan:
1. **Wrap the pan:** Wrap the outside of a 9-inch springform pan with 2-3 layers of heavy-duty aluminum foil to prevent water from seeping in during the water bath.

2. **Preheat oven:** Preheat your oven to 325°F (163°C).

### Make the Crust:
1. **Mix ingredients:** In a medium bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs, sugar, salt, and melted butter. Mix until the texture resembles wet sand.

2. **Press into pan:** Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of the prepared springform pan. Use the bottom of a glass to pack it down evenly.

3. **Pre-bake:** Bake for 10 minutes. Remove and let cool while you prepare the filling.

### Prepare the Coffee Syrup:
1. **Combine ingredients:** Mix the cooled espresso, coffee liqueur (if using), and sugar in a shallow bowl. Stir until sugar dissolves. Set aside.

### Make the Cheesecake Filling:
1. **Beat cream cheese and mascarpone:** In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese and mascarpone together with an electric mixer on medium speed until smooth and creamy (about 3 minutes).

2. **Add sugar and flour:** Add the sugar and flour, beating until well combined.

3. **Add flavorings:** Mix in the vanilla extract and instant espresso powder until fully incorporated.

4. **Add eggs:** Add the eggs one at a time, beating on low speed after each addition just until combined. Don't overmix.

5. **Add sour cream:** Gently fold in the sour cream until smooth.

### Assemble the Cheesecake:
1. **Dip ladyfingers:** Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the coffee syrup for 1-2 seconds per side (don't oversoak or they'll fall apart).

2. **Layer ladyfingers:** Arrange the coffee-soaked ladyfingers in a single layer over the cooled crust, breaking them as needed to fit.

3. **Add filling:** Pour the cheesecake filling over the ladyfinger layer and spread evenly with a spatula.

4. **Remove air bubbles:** Gently tap the pan on the counter several times to release air bubbles.

### Bake:
1. **Prepare water bath:** Place the foil-wrapped springform pan in a large roasting pan. Pour hot water into the roasting pan until it reaches about halfway up the sides of the springform pan.

2. **Bake:** Bake for 1 hour 15 minutes, or until the edges are set but the center still has a slight jiggle (about 3 inches in diameter).

3. **Turn off oven:** Turn off the oven and crack the door open slightly. Let the cheesecake cool in the oven for 1 hour.

4. **Cool completely:** Remove from the water bath and cool completely at room temperature on a wire rack.

### Chill and Serve:
1. **Refrigerate:** Cover the cheesecake with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 6-8 hours, or preferably overnight.

2. **Dust with cocoa:** Before serving, place cocoa powder in a fine-mesh sieve and dust the top of the cheesecake generously.

3. **Release from pan:** Run a thin knife around the edge before releasing the springform pan sides.

4. **Garnish:** Add chocolate shavings and whipped cream if desired.

5. **Slice and serve:** Use a sharp knife dipped in hot water and wiped clean between cuts for neat slices.

## Tips for Success

- **Room temperature ingredients:** This is crucial! Cold ingredients can cause lumps and prevent proper incorporation.

- **Don't overmix:** Once you add the eggs, mix just until combined to prevent cracks and a dense texture.

- **Water bath is key:** The water bath (bain-marie) ensures gentle, even cooking and prevents cracks.

- **Check for doneness:** The cheesecake should jiggle slightly in the center when done. It will firm up as it cools.

- **Don't skip the chill time:** The cheesecake needs adequate time to set properly and develop flavors.

- **Prevent cracks:** Avoid opening the oven door during baking, and allow gradual cooling.

- **Storage:** Cover and refrigerate for up to 5 days.

- **Make ahead:** This dessert is perfect for making a day or two ahead. The flavors actually improve with time!

## Variations

- **Chocolate version:** Add 4 oz melted dark chocolate to the filling
- **Amaretto twist:** Substitute amaretto liqueur for the coffee liqueur
- **Lighter version:** Use 1/3 less fat cream cheese and low-fat mascarpone
- **No-bake option:** Skip the baking and use a gelatin-stabilized filling instead

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve with a shot of espresso on the side
- Drizzle with chocolate sauce
- Top with fresh whipped cream and chocolate-covered espresso beans
- Pair with a glass of sweet dessert wine like Vin Santo

Buon appetito! Enjoy your sophisticated tiramisu cheesecake!
