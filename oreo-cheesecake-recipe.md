# Oreo Cheesecake Recipe

A decadent no-bake Oreo cheesecake with a crunchy cookie crust and creamy filling loaded with Oreo pieces.

## Prep Time
- **Prep:** 30 minutes
- **Chill:** 4-6 hours (or overnight)
- **Total:** 4 hours 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 12 slices

## Ingredients

### For the Crust:
- 24 Oreo cookies (about 2 cups crushed)
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted

### For the Filling:
- 24 oz (3 packages) cream cheese, softened to room temperature
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup sour cream, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1½ cups heavy whipping cream, cold
- 20 Oreo cookies, roughly chopped
- Extra Oreos for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

### Prepare the Crust:
1. **Crush the cookies:** Place 24 Oreos (including the cream filling) in a food processor and pulse until fine crumbs form. Alternatively, place them in a sealed plastic bag and crush with a rolling pin.

2. **Mix with butter:** Transfer the cookie crumbs to a bowl and stir in the melted butter until the mixture resembles wet sand.

3. **Press into pan:** Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan, creating an even layer. Use the bottom of a glass or measuring cup to pack it down firmly.

4. **Chill:** Place the crust in the refrigerator while you prepare the filling.

### Make the Filling:
1. **Beat cream cheese:** In a large bowl, beat the softened cream cheese with an electric mixer on medium speed until smooth and fluffy (about 2-3 minutes).

2. **Add sugar:** Add the sugar and beat for another 2 minutes until well combined and creamy.

3. **Add sour cream and vanilla:** Mix in the sour cream and vanilla extract until smooth. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed.

4. **Whip the cream:** In a separate bowl, whip the cold heavy cream with an electric mixer on high speed until stiff peaks form (about 3-4 minutes).

5. **Fold together:** Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture in three additions, being careful not to deflate the mixture.

6. **Add Oreos:** Fold in the chopped Oreo cookies, reserving a few pieces for topping if desired.

### Assemble:
1. **Pour filling:** Pour the cheesecake filling over the prepared crust and spread evenly with a spatula.

2. **Smooth the top:** Gently tap the pan on the counter a few times to release any air bubbles and smooth the top.

3. **Garnish:** If desired, arrange Oreo halves or quarters on top of the cheesecake in a decorative pattern.

4. **Chill:** Cover the cheesecake with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours, or preferably overnight, until firm and set.

### Serve:
1. **Release from pan:** Run a knife around the edge of the cheesecake before releasing the springform pan sides.

2. **Slice:** Use a sharp knife dipped in hot water and wiped clean between cuts for neat slices.

3. **Enjoy:** Serve chilled and enjoy your delicious Oreo cheesecake!

## Tips for Success

- **Room temperature ingredients:** Make sure the cream cheese and sour cream are at room temperature for a smooth, lump-free filling.

- **Don't overbeat:** Once you add the whipped cream, fold gently to maintain the light, airy texture.

- **Chill time:** Don't skip the chilling time! The cheesecake needs adequate time to set properly.

- **Storage:** Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

- **Freezing:** This cheesecake freezes well! Wrap tightly and freeze for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before serving.

## Variations

- **Chocolate drizzle:** Drizzle melted chocolate over the top before serving
- **Cookie butter:** Add 2 tablespoons of cookie butter to the filling for extra flavor
- **Mini cheesecakes:** Make individual servings in a muffin tin lined with cupcake liners

Enjoy your homemade Oreo cheesecake!
