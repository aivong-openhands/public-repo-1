# Chocolate Cheesecake Recipe

## Ingredients

### For the Crust:
- 2 cups (200g) chocolate cookie crumbs (about 24 Oreos, filling removed)
- 5 tablespoons (70g) unsalted butter, melted
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar

### For the Filling:
- 24 oz (680g) cream cheese, softened to room temperature
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 cup (240g) sour cream, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 3 large eggs, at room temperature
- 8 oz (225g) semi-sweet chocolate, melted and slightly cooled

### For the Ganache Topping (optional):
- 6 oz (170g) semi-sweet chocolate, chopped
- ½ cup (120ml) heavy cream

## Instructions

### Prepare the Crust:
1. Preheat oven to 325°F (163°C).
2. Mix cookie crumbs, melted butter, and sugar until combined.
3. Press mixture firmly into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan.
4. Bake for 10 minutes, then remove and let cool.

### Make the Filling:
1. Beat cream cheese on medium speed until smooth and creamy (2-3 minutes).
2. Add sugar and flour, beat until combined.
3. Mix in sour cream and vanilla extract.
4. Add eggs one at a time, beating on low speed after each addition (don't overmix).
5. Pour in melted chocolate and mix until fully incorporated.
6. Pour filling over the crust.

### Bake:
1. Wrap the outside of the pan with aluminum foil (to prevent water leaking in).
2. Place springform pan in a larger roasting pan. Pour hot water into the roasting pan (water bath) until it reaches halfway up the sides of the springform pan.
3. Bake for 60-75 minutes, until the edges are set but the center still jiggles slightly.
4. Turn off oven and crack the door open. Let cheesecake cool in the oven for 1 hour.
5. Remove from oven and water bath. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours (preferably overnight).

### Make the Ganache (optional):
1. Heat cream until just simmering, pour over chopped chocolate.
2. Let sit for 2 minutes, then whisk until smooth.
3. Pour over chilled cheesecake and spread evenly.
4. Refrigerate until ganache sets (about 30 minutes).

## Tips:
- All ingredients should be at room temperature for a smooth filling
- Don't overmix after adding eggs to prevent cracks
- The water bath helps prevent cracks and ensures even baking
- Let cheesecake chill completely for best texture and slicing

**Serves:** 12-16 slices  
**Total Time:** 6+ hours (including chilling)

Enjoy your rich, creamy chocolate cheesecake!
