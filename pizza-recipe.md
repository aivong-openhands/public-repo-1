# Classic Homemade Pizza Recipe

## Ingredients

### For the Dough:
- 3 ½ cups (440g) all-purpose flour
- 1 packet (2 ¼ tsp) active dry yeast
- 1 ½ cups (355ml) warm water (110°F/45°C)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons salt

### For the Sauce:
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the Toppings:
- 2 cups (200g) shredded mozzarella cheese
- Fresh basil leaves
- Additional toppings of your choice (pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, onions, olives, etc.)

## Instructions

### Making the Dough:

1. **Activate the yeast**: In a small bowl, combine warm water and sugar. Sprinkle yeast on top and let it sit for 5-10 minutes until foamy.

2. **Mix the dough**: In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Make a well in the center and add the yeast mixture and olive oil.

3. **Knead**: Mix until a dough forms, then knead on a floured surface for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic.

4. **First rise**: Place dough in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp towel, and let rise in a warm place for 1-2 hours until doubled in size.

### Making the Sauce:

1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper.
4. Simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Let cool before using.

### Assembling and Baking:

1. **Preheat oven**: Heat oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven while preheating.

2. **Shape the dough**: Punch down the dough and divide into 2 portions. Roll out each portion into a 12-inch circle on a floured surface.

3. **Add toppings**: Transfer dough to a pizza pan or parchment paper. Spread sauce evenly, leaving a ½-inch border. Add cheese and your favorite toppings.

4. **Bake**: Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.

5. **Finish**: Remove from oven, add fresh basil leaves, let cool for 2-3 minutes, slice, and serve!

## Tips

- For a crispier crust, prebake the dough for 3-4 minutes before adding toppings.
- Don't overload with toppings - less is more for a crispy pizza.
- Let the dough come to room temperature before shaping for easier handling.
- Brush the crust edges with olive oil and garlic for extra flavor.

## Yield
Makes 2 large pizzas (12 inches each)

Enjoy your homemade pizza! 🍕
