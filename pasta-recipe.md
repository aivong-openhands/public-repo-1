# Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

A traditional Italian pasta dish from Rome, featuring a creamy sauce made without cream!

## Ingredients

### For 4 servings:
- 400g (14 oz) spaghetti
- 200g (7 oz) guanciale or pancetta, diced
- 4 large egg yolks
- 1 whole egg
- 100g (1 cup) Pecorino Romano cheese, finely grated
- 50g (½ cup) Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, finely grated
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Salt for pasta water

## Instructions

### Preparation:
1. **Prepare the cheese mixture**: In a large bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, whole egg, and both grated cheeses. Add a generous amount of freshly ground black pepper. Set aside.

2. **Cook the guanciale**: In a large skillet over medium heat, add the diced guanciale. Cook for 8-10 minutes until the fat renders and the meat becomes crispy. Remove from heat but keep the rendered fat in the pan.

3. **Cook the pasta**: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook until al dente (usually 1-2 minutes less than package directions). Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water before draining.

### Assembly:
4. **Combine**: Add the drained pasta directly to the skillet with the guanciale (off heat). Toss to coat the pasta with the rendered fat.

5. **Create the sauce**: Add about ¼ cup of the reserved pasta water to the egg and cheese mixture, whisking quickly to temper the eggs. Pour this mixture over the pasta and toss vigorously. The residual heat will cook the eggs gently, creating a creamy sauce. Add more pasta water as needed to achieve a silky consistency.

6. **Serve immediately**: Plate the carbonara and finish with extra grated Pecorino Romano and black pepper.

## Tips

- **Temperature is key**: The pan should be warm but not hot when adding the egg mixture to prevent scrambling.
- **Work quickly**: The sauce comes together fast, so have everything ready before you start combining.
- **Pasta water is magic**: The starchy water helps emulsify the sauce and create that perfect creamy texture.
- **Traditional choice**: Guanciale (cured pork jowl) is traditional, but pancetta or bacon can be substituted.
- **No cream**: Authentic carbonara never contains cream - the creaminess comes from the eggs and cheese!

## Variations

- **Vegetarian**: Replace guanciale with mushrooms sautéed in olive oil and add smoked paprika for depth.
- **Lighter version**: Use 2 whole eggs and 2 yolks instead of 4 yolks and 1 whole egg.
- **Extra vegetables**: Some like to add peas or asparagus, though this isn't traditional.

---

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes  
**Total Time:** 30 minutes
