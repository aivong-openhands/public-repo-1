# Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

## Ingredients

### For 4 servings:
- 400g spaghetti
- 200g guanciale (or pancetta), diced
- 4 large egg yolks
- 1 whole egg
- 100g Pecorino Romano cheese, finely grated
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Salt for pasta water

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the ingredients**
   - Bring a large pot of salted water to boil
   - Dice the guanciale into small cubes
   - Mix egg yolks and whole egg in a bowl
   - Grate the Pecorino Romano cheese finely

2. **Cook the guanciale**
   - In a large skillet over medium heat, cook the guanciale until crispy and golden (about 5-7 minutes)
   - Remove from heat and set aside, keeping the rendered fat in the pan

3. **Cook the pasta**
   - Add spaghetti to boiling water
   - Cook until al dente (usually 1 minute less than package directions)
   - Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water before draining

4. **Make the sauce**
   - In the bowl with eggs, add the grated Pecorino Romano
   - Add generous amounts of freshly ground black pepper
   - Mix well to create a smooth paste

5. **Combine everything**
   - Add the drained pasta to the skillet with guanciale
   - Remove skillet from heat (important!)
   - Quickly add the egg and cheese mixture
   - Toss vigorously, adding pasta water little by little until creamy
   - The residual heat will cook the eggs without scrambling them

6. **Serve immediately**
   - Plate the carbonara
   - Add extra Pecorino Romano and black pepper on top
   - Serve hot

## Tips

- **Temperature is key**: The pan should be off the heat when adding the egg mixture to prevent scrambling
- **No cream**: Traditional carbonara doesn't use cream - the creaminess comes from the eggs and cheese
- **Pasta water is magic**: The starchy pasta water helps emulsify the sauce
- **Work quickly**: Carbonara waits for no one - serve immediately while hot and creamy

## Variations

- Substitute guanciale with pancetta or bacon if unavailable
- Try adding a splash of white wine to deglaze the pan after cooking the guanciale
- Mix Pecorino Romano with Parmigiano Reggiano for a milder flavor
