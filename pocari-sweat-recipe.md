# Pocari Sweat Recipe

Pocari Sweat is a popular Japanese sports drink known for its light, refreshing taste and electrolyte-replenishing properties. This homemade version mimics the original formula.

## Ingredients

- 1 liter (4 cups) water
- 4 tablespoons sugar (50g)
- 1/4 teaspoon salt (1.5g)
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda (sodium bicarbonate)
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice (fresh or bottled)
- 1/4 teaspoon citric acid (optional, for tartness)
- A pinch of potassium chloride or cream of tartar (optional, for extra electrolytes)

## Instructions

1. **Heat the water**: Warm about 1/4 of the water (don't boil, just warm enough to dissolve ingredients).

2. **Dissolve dry ingredients**: Add the sugar, salt, and baking soda to the warm water. Stir until completely dissolved.

3. **Add lemon juice**: Pour in the lemon juice and citric acid (if using). Mix well.

4. **Add remaining water**: Pour in the rest of the cold water and stir to combine.

5. **Chill**: Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.

6. **Serve**: Pour over ice and enjoy! Best consumed cold.

## Notes

- **Electrolyte Balance**: The combination of salt, baking soda, and optional potassium provides sodium, bicarbonate, and potassium - key electrolytes lost during exercise.

- **Sweetness Level**: Adjust sugar to taste. The original Pocari Sweat is moderately sweet but not overly so.

- **Storage**: Keep refrigerated and consume within 3-4 days.

- **Flavor Variations**: You can add a tiny amount of grapefruit juice for a more complex citrus flavor.

## When to Drink

- After exercise or sports activities
- During hot weather to prevent dehydration
- When recovering from illness
- As a hangover remedy (popular use in Japan!)

## Nutritional Benefits

This homemade sports drink helps:
- Replenish electrolytes lost through sweat
- Maintain hydration more effectively than water alone
- Provide quick energy through simple sugars
- Support recovery after physical activity

Enjoy your homemade Pocari Sweat! 🥤
