# Almond Cookies Recipe

A delicious and crispy almond cookie recipe with a perfect nutty flavor.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1 cup finely ground almonds
- 1 teaspoon almond extract
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Whole almonds for decoration (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Preheat the oven** to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.

2. **Prepare the dough**: In a large bowl, cream together the softened butter, granulated sugar, and powdered sugar until light and fluffy (about 3-4 minutes).

3. **Add flavorings**: Mix in the almond extract and vanilla extract until well combined.

4. **Combine dry ingredients**: In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, ground almonds, and salt.

5. **Mix the dough**: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, mixing until just combined. Do not overmix.

6. **Shape the cookies**: Roll the dough into 1-inch balls and place them 2 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets. Gently flatten each ball slightly with your palm or the bottom of a glass.

7. **Add decoration**: If desired, press a whole almond into the center of each cookie.

8. **Bake**: Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden. The centers may still look slightly soft.

9. **Cool**: Allow cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Tips

- For extra almond flavor, you can add 1/4 cup sliced almonds to the dough.
- Store cookies in an airtight container for up to 1 week.
- These cookies freeze well for up to 3 months.
- If you prefer a sweeter cookie, dust with powdered sugar after cooling.

## Yield

Makes approximately 24-30 cookies

## Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Total Time: 30 minutes
