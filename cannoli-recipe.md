# Classic Sicilian Cannoli Recipe

## Ingredients

### For the Shells:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons butter, cold and cubed
- 1 egg yolk
- 1/2 cup dry white wine or Marsala wine
- Vegetable oil for frying
- 1 egg white (for sealing)

### For the Filling:
- 2 cups ricotta cheese (preferably whole milk)
- 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips
- 2 tablespoons candied orange peel, finely chopped (optional)
- Pinch of cinnamon

### For Garnish:
- Powdered sugar for dusting
- Chopped pistachios
- Mini chocolate chips
- Candied cherries (optional)

## Instructions

### Making the Shells:

1. **Prepare the dough**: In a large bowl, combine flour, sugar, and salt. Cut in the cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.

2. **Add wet ingredients**: Make a well in the center and add the egg yolk and wine. Mix with a fork, then knead by hand until a smooth dough forms (about 10 minutes).

3. **Rest the dough**: Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (or up to 24 hours).

4. **Roll out the dough**: On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough very thin (about 1/16 inch thick). Cut into 4-inch circles using a round cutter or glass.

5. **Shape the shells**: Wrap each circle around a cannoli tube, sealing the edge with egg white. Make sure the dough overlaps slightly.

6. **Fry the shells**: Heat oil to 350°F (175°C) in a deep pot. Fry 2-3 shells at a time until golden brown (about 2-3 minutes). Remove carefully and let cool on paper towels before sliding off the tubes.

### Making the Filling:

1. **Drain the ricotta**: If your ricotta is watery, place it in a cheesecloth-lined strainer over a bowl and refrigerate for 2-3 hours to drain excess moisture.

2. **Mix the filling**: In a large bowl, combine drained ricotta, powdered sugar, vanilla, and cinnamon. Beat until smooth and creamy.

3. **Add mix-ins**: Fold in chocolate chips and candied orange peel (if using). Refrigerate until ready to fill.

### Assembly:

1. **Fill the shells**: Using a piping bag or spoon, fill each shell from both ends, working toward the center. Fill just before serving to keep shells crispy.

2. **Garnish**: Dip the ends in chopped pistachios or mini chocolate chips. Dust with powdered sugar.

3. **Serve immediately**: Cannoli are best enjoyed fresh, within a few hours of filling.

## Tips

- Keep shells and filling separate until serving time to maintain crispiness
- For authentic flavor, use sheep's milk ricotta if available
- The dough can be made ahead and frozen for up to 3 months
- Cannoli tubes can be reused; metal tubes work best
- If you don't have cannoli tubes, you can make smaller "cannoncini" by wrapping around wooden dowels

## Yield
Makes approximately 12-15 cannoli
