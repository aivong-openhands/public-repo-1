# Classic Macaroni and Cheese Recipe

A creamy, comforting classic macaroni and cheese that's perfect for any occasion.

## Ingredients

### For the pasta:
- 1 lb (450g) elbow macaroni
- 2 tablespoons salt (for pasta water)

### For the cheese sauce:
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 cups whole milk (warmed)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3 cups sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- 1 cup gruyere cheese, shredded
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon mustard powder
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)

### For the topping (optional):
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the Pasta
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil
2. Add the macaroni and cook according to package directions until al dente (usually 7-8 minutes)
3. Drain and set aside

### Step 2: Make the Cheese Sauce
1. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat
2. Add the flour and whisk constantly for 2-3 minutes to create a roux
3. Gradually add the warm milk and cream, whisking constantly to prevent lumps
4. Continue cooking and stirring until the mixture thickens (about 5 minutes)
5. Reduce heat to low and add the shredded cheddar and gruyere cheese
6. Stir until all cheese is melted and smooth
7. Add garlic powder, mustard powder, paprika, salt, pepper, and cayenne
8. Stir to combine

### Step 3: Combine
1. Add the cooked macaroni to the cheese sauce
2. Stir gently until all pasta is evenly coated
3. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed

### Step 4: Optional Baked Version
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
2. Transfer mac and cheese to a greased 9x13 inch baking dish
3. Mix panko breadcrumbs with melted butter and parmesan
4. Sprinkle the breadcrumb mixture over the top
5. Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden and bubbly

## Serving Suggestions
- Serve immediately while hot and creamy
- Pairs well with roasted vegetables or a fresh green salad
- Great as a side dish or main course

## Tips
- Use freshly shredded cheese for the smoothest sauce (pre-shredded cheese contains anti-caking agents)
- Don't overcook the pasta - it should be slightly firm as it will continue to soften in the sauce
- Warm the milk before adding to prevent the sauce from cooling down
- Add a splash of milk if reheating leftovers to restore creaminess

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes  
**Total Time:** 30 minutes  
**Servings:** 6-8 people

Enjoy your homemade macaroni and cheese!
