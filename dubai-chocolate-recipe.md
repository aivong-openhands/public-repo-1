# Dubai Chocolate Recipe

## Description
Dubai chocolate is a viral chocolate treat featuring a crispy, crunchy pistachio and kadayif (shredded phyllo dough) filling encased in smooth chocolate. This luxurious dessert combines Middle Eastern flavors with rich chocolate for an unforgettable taste experience.

## Ingredients

### For the Chocolate Shell:
- 400g (14 oz) high-quality milk or dark chocolate (or a combination)
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil (optional, for smoother texture)

### For the Pistachio Kadayif Filling:
- 200g (7 oz) kadayif (shredded phyllo dough/kataifi)
- 100g (3.5 oz) unsalted butter
- 150g (5.3 oz) pistachio cream or paste
- 100g (3.5 oz) crushed pistachios
- 2 tablespoons tahini (optional)
- 2 tablespoons honey or sugar
- Pinch of salt

## Equipment
- Chocolate bar mold or rectangular silicone mold
- Double boiler or microwave-safe bowl
- Baking sheet
- Parchment paper

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Kadayif Filling
1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Roughly chop the kadayif into smaller pieces (about 2-3 cm long).
3. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
4. Add the chopped kadayif to the melted butter and toss until well coated.
5. Spread the kadayif on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
6. Bake for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and crispy.
7. Let it cool completely.

### Step 2: Make the Pistachio Filling
1. In a bowl, combine the cooled crispy kadayif, pistachio cream, crushed pistachios, tahini (if using), honey, and a pinch of salt.
2. Mix well until everything is evenly combined. The mixture should be thick and hold together.
3. Set aside.

### Step 3: Temper the Chocolate
1. Chop the chocolate into small, uniform pieces.
2. Melt 3/4 of the chocolate using a double boiler or microwave (in 30-second intervals, stirring between each).
3. Once melted and smooth (45-50°C/113-122°F), remove from heat.
4. Add the remaining 1/4 of the chocolate and stir until fully melted and cooled to about 27-28°C (80-82°F).
5. Gently reheat to 31-32°C (88-90°F) for working temperature.
6. If using coconut oil, add it when the chocolate is melted and mix well.

### Step 4: Assemble the Dubai Chocolate
1. Pour a layer of melted chocolate into your mold, ensuring it coats the bottom and sides evenly (about 1/4 inch thick).
2. Use a spoon or brush to spread the chocolate up the sides.
3. Place the mold in the refrigerator for 5-10 minutes until the chocolate is set.
4. Remove from the fridge and add the pistachio kadayif filling, leaving space at the top for the final chocolate layer.
5. Pour the remaining melted chocolate over the filling to seal it completely.
6. Gently tap the mold on the counter to remove air bubbles and smooth the surface.
7. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours or until completely set.

### Step 5: Unmold and Serve
1. Once fully set, carefully remove the chocolate from the mold.
2. If needed, let it sit at room temperature for a few minutes to make unmolding easier.
3. Cut into bars or pieces as desired.

## Tips
- Use high-quality chocolate for the best flavor and texture.
- If you can't find kadayif, you can substitute with crushed phyllo pastry or even rice cereal for crunch.
- Store in an airtight container in a cool place for up to 2 weeks.
- For a dairy-free version, use dark chocolate and replace butter with coconut oil.
- The filling can be customized with other nuts like hazelnuts or almonds.

## Serving Suggestions
Serve at room temperature for the best texture and flavor. The contrast between the smooth chocolate and the crunchy pistachio filling makes each bite spectacular!

Enjoy your homemade Dubai chocolate! 🍫
